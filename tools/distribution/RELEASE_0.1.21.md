# WF Companion 0.1.21

Arrange your Overview character cards the way you like. Drag a character name or its six-dot grip onto another card to move it. The name follows the pointer, the original card dims and the destination border highlights. Keyboard users can focus a name and press Ctrl+Left or Ctrl+Right.

Card order is remembered independently of character profiles and plans. New characters append after your arranged cards. Hide/Show in the Your characters header collapses the section to its title, and that choice is also remembered across refreshes and restarts.

Automatic save discovery pauses during a drag. Your manual plans, saved characters and game files are preserved. Optional addon packages and inventory eligibility rules are unchanged. No optical bridge is included.

A smaller update from 0.1.20 is offered when the installed files match its base; the full installer remains available as a fallback. Publisher signing remains pending.
