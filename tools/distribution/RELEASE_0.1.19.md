# WF Companion 0.1.19

Remember your Beta saved-character connection across restarts.

After updating, open **Overview > Read professions save**, choose your Beta account's **SavedVariables** folder, then click **Connect & remember characters** once. The companion will read that folder when it starts, restore saved professions and link verified saved characters automatically. Existing manual characters and plans are kept; profiles are matched by source and GUID, not by name.

Use **Details > Forget saved connection** to stop startup reads without deleting characters or plans. Unavailable or invalid saves produce an attention message. These are saved observations, not live character tracking. Inventory eligibility checks remain separate and unchanged.

This update uses the full installer fallback because it adds an application module. Existing personal settings remain outside the installation folder. Optional addon packages are unchanged; the parked optical bridge is not included. The installer remains unsigned while publisher signing is pending.
