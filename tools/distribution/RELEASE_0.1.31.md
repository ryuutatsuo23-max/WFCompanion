# WF Companion 0.1.31

Settings now includes companion backup and restore. Create a ZIP of your saved companion settings, character profiles, manual inventory and plans, then preview its date and characters before restoring.

Cancel leaves your data unchanged. Restore creates a recovery backup first and closes the companion; reopen it to load the restored records. Damaged or unsupported backups are rejected. Interrupted restores recover before startup.

Save your edits first. Backups contain personal character names and file paths; keep them private. Game saves, addon files and unfinished editor drafts are not included. Missing game-save connections can be reconnected in Settings.

The addon bundle is unchanged from 0.1.30. Saved inventory remains read-only and manual plans stay separate. Beta has ended; outstanding live and release-client checks remain pending.

The installer is unsigned. SHA-256 checksums and dependency source archives accompany this release.
